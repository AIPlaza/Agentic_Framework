Domain ERD (high-level)

Users (1) ---< Projects (N)
Projects (1) ---< LogicalFramework (1)
Projects (1) ---< Indicators (N)
Projects (1) ---< Checklists (N)
Checklists (1) ---< ChecklistEntries (N)
Projects (1) ---< OracleReadings (N)
Projects (1) ---< EvaluatorReports (N)
Indicators (1) ---< FnvCTranches (N)
Projects (1) ---< MarketListings (0..1)
AuditLogs: append-only, references entities by (entity_type, entity_id)

Key entities and important fields:
- users: id, email, role
- projects: id, owner_id, title, status, dev_level
- logical_framework: project_id, impact, outcomes[], outputs[], activities[]
- indicators: project_id, name, racer JSON, fnvc_eligible, usd_value, verification_source
- checklists/checklist_entries: schema JSON + values JSON, submitted_by, approved_by
- oracle_readings: source, raw_value, normalized_value, validated
- evaluator_reports: evaluator_id, report_json, status, approved_at
- fnvc_tranches: indicator_id, threshold, payout_usd, paid
- market_listings: project_id, public_slug, scenarios JSON
